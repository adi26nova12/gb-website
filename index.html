        The Confluence of Modernity, Mystique & Legacy

        New Chandigarh.

        More than an extension of Chandigarh — a bold reimagination of the City Beautiful’s legacy. Nestled at the foothills of the Lower Shivaliks, it blends Chandigarh’s signature planned aesthetics with contemporary innovation: serene yet connected, and emerging as a metropolis that rivals India’s greatest urban centres.

        Where colossal infrastructure converges with prestigious educational institutions, medical facilities and hospitality, New Chandigarh is where it all leads.

        Where landmarks rise from the cityscape — a life both vibrant and tranquil.
